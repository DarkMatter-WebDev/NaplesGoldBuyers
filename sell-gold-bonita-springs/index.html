Getting a Fair Number

Get more than one offer. We mean that.

Most advice about selling gold is vague because most things people sell are hard to price. Gold isn't. It trades on a published market every business day, which means two offers on the same chain are directly comparable in a way that two offers on a used car never are.

So shop it. Take your pieces to two or three buyers and ask each of them the same three questions:

  • What does it weigh? In grams, on a scale you can see.
  • What karat did you test it at? And how did you test it?
  • What spot price are you using today? It should match the published market.

Any buyer who won't answer all three is asking you to trust a number with no reasoning attached. We'd rather you come back to us after checking two other shops than sell to us wondering. A meaningful share of our business is people who did exactly that.
