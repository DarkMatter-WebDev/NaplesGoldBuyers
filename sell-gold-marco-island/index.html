The House Call

The appraisal happens at your kitchen table

Marco is a thirty-minute drive from our Naples showroom, and for most people the trip isn't worth making twice — once to get a number, once to sell. So we bring the showroom to you: a calibrated scale, an electronic tester, a touchstone and acids, and a loupe. Everything is tested in front of you, on your table, with the arithmetic written out.

There's no charge for the visit and no minimum. If you have one chain, we'll still come. If you decide the number isn't for you, we shake hands and leave — that happens regularly and it's a perfectly normal outcome.

Condo buildings and gated communities

A good share of Marco is buildings with a front desk. If yours prefers that visitors meet residents in the lobby or a clubhouse room, that works — the equipment is portable and the appraisal takes about fifteen minutes. Tell us the building when you call and we'll handle gate registration or advance notice in whatever way your association requires.

Seasonal residents: call early, not in your last week

Late spring is consistently our busiest stretch for island visits, because people decide to deal with a jewelry box in the days before closing up and heading north. If you already know you'll be selling before you leave, a call in February beats a call the week of your flight — you'll have time to shop the offer against other buyers, which is what we'd recommend you do anyway.

Estates and downsizing

Much of what we're asked to look at on Marco isn't one piece — it's an inherited collection, or the contents of a home being sold. Those visits run longer and we plan for them differently, so mention it when you call. We handle gold, sterling flatware and holloware, coin collections, diamonds, and luxury watches, and we're comfortable working alongside an estate attorney or executor when one is involved.

What We Buy

Bring out everything before we arrive

Gold jewelry

10k through 24k, stamped or not, broken or intact. Condition doesn't change scrap value — don't repair anything first.

Coins & bullion

Krugerrands, Eagles, Maple Leafs, Sovereigns, bars and rounds. Collectible coins are valued above melt where the numismatic premium is real.

Sterling silver

Flatware services, tea sets, trays, and holloware. Weight matters more than pattern for most sterling.

Watches & diamonds

Rolex, Cartier, Omega, Patek and others, plus loose and mounted stones. Bring boxes and papers if you still have them.

FAQ

Marco Island questions

Do you actually come to Marco Island?
+

Yes, regularly. No charge for the visit, no minimum, no obligation. We bring the scale and testing equipment and appraise everything in front of you.

Can we meet in my building's lobby or clubhouse?
+

Absolutely — many Marco buildings prefer it, and the equipment is fully portable. Let us know the building when you call so we can handle gate registration or advance notice.

I'm only here for the season. When should I call?
+

Earlier than you think. Late spring gets busy with people closing up for the year. Calling with a few weeks to spare also gives you time to compare our offer against other buyers.

Is it safe to have a buyer come to the house?
+

You choose the time and place, family is welcome to sit in, and nothing leaves your hands unless you accept an offer. We're a licensed local business with a Naples storefront and verified Google reviews. If you'd rather meet somewhere neutral, that's fine.

What do I need to have ready?
+

The items and a photo ID — Florida law requires identification for precious metal purchases. Everything else is on us.
